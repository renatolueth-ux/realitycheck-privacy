🔒 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RealityCheck-Timer handles data. RealityCheck-Timer is designed to store app data locally on the user's device and to use Meta Horizon platform features only where required for in-app purchases.

Last updated: May 15, 2026

📱 App Data Stored Locally

RealityCheck-Timer stores certain app data locally on the user's device in order to provide its core functionality.

This may include:

  • Timer settings, such as timer duration and selected timer mode
  • Interval configurations and training plans
  • Custom user-created training plans
  • Training statistics, such as training date, duration, and plan name
  • Stopwatch state and timer progress
  • Trial start time and remaining trial status
  • Local purchase unlock status, stored as a simple true/false value
  • App preferences, such as display or settings choices

This data is stored locally on the device. RealityCheck-Timer does not upload this data to our own servers.

🛒 Meta Horizon In-App Purchases

RealityCheck-Timer uses Meta Horizon in-app purchase functionality to allow users to purchase and unlock the full version of the app.

When the app checks purchase status, it requests purchase entitlement information from Meta for the currently logged-in Meta user. This is used only to determine whether the user has purchased the full unlock add-on and to unlock or restore purchased functionality.

The app may process in-app purchase information provided by Meta, such as whether the currently logged-in Meta user owns the RealityCheck-Timer full unlock add-on.

RealityCheck-Timer does not use this information for advertising, tracking, profiling, or sale to third parties.

👤 Meta User Profile Data

RealityCheck-Timer does not access or use the user's Meta profile name, profile picture, friends list, email address, or other social profile information.

If Meta internally associates an in-app purchase or entitlement check with the currently logged-in Meta user, this is used only for purchase verification and unlocking purchased app functionality.

💾 Backup and Restore

RealityCheck-Timer includes a backup and restore function. Users can export and import their own training plans and training statistics.

Backup files are created only when the user chooses to export them. The user selects the storage location through the Android system file picker.

Restore files are selected by the user through the Android system file picker. RealityCheck-Timer does not automatically upload backup files to our servers.

🔔 Notifications and Alarms

RealityCheck-Timer uses local notifications and alarms to notify users when timers or intervals are completed.

Notification content may include timer or interval names selected or created by the user. These notifications are generated locally on the device.

🌐 Internet Access and Servers

RealityCheck-Timer does not operate its own user account system and does not upload user-created timer, plan, or statistics data to our own servers.

Internet or platform connectivity may be used by Meta Horizon platform services for in-app purchase and entitlement verification.

🚫 No Advertising or Tracking

RealityCheck-Timer does not use user data for advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics profiling, or sale to third parties.

The app does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

🗑️ Data Deletion

Because RealityCheck-Timer stores app data locally on the user's device, users can delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data in the device system settings.

Users may also delete or overwrite training plans and statistics within the app, where such functions are available.

If you would like to request deletion of app-related data or have questions about your data, please contact us at:

🧒 Children

RealityCheck-Timer is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not operate its own account system and does not request personal information such as name, email address, or location.
