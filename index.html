🔒 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RealityCheck-Timer processes limited platform-related data required for app functionality and Meta Horizon platform services.

📦 Local App Data

RealityCheck-Timer stores app-related data locally on the user's device.

This may include:

  • Timer settings
  • Interval configurations
  • Training plans
  • Training statistics
  • Trial status
  • Purchase unlock status

This locally stored data is not uploaded to external developer-owned servers.

🛒 Meta Horizon In-App Purchases

RealityCheck-Timer uses Meta Horizon In-App Purchases (IAP) to allow users to unlock the full version of the app after the trial period.

The app uses Meta purchase entitlement verification to determine whether the currently logged-in Meta user owns the “RealityCheck Full Unlock” add-on.

This purchase verification may involve processing limited Meta platform account-related information such as:

  • Meta User ID
  • Meta platform entitlement status
  • Purchase ownership information

This functionality is used exclusively for purchase verification, restoring purchases, and unlocking paid app functionality.

👤 Meta User Profile Data

RealityCheck-Timer does not independently display, store, sell, or share the user's Meta profile name, profile picture, friends list, email address, or other social profile information.

Meta Horizon platform services may internally process certain user profile-related information in connection with in-app purchases, entitlement verification, or platform account authentication.

Any such processing is used only for purchase verification and unlocking purchased app functionality.

RealityCheck-Timer does not use Meta user profile data for advertising, tracking, analytics profiling, or sharing with third parties.

🚫 No Advertising or Tracking

RealityCheck-Timer does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, analytics services, or third-party marketing tools.

The app does not sell user data and does not share user data with third parties for advertising purposes.

💾 Backup and Restore

The app allows users to create and restore local backup files containing app-related data such as training plans and statistics.

Backup files are created and managed locally by the user and are not uploaded to external servers by RealityCheck-Timer.

🗑 Data Deletion

Users can delete locally stored app data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app storage in the device settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Deleting locally created backup files manually

Users may also contact the developer regarding privacy-related requests.

🧒 Children

RealityCheck-Timer does not operate its own account system and does not request users to manually provide personal information such as real name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Certain Meta Horizon platform features, including in-app purchase and entitlement services, may process limited account-related platform data as required for platform functionality.
